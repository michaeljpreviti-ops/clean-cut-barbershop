About us

        
Earl opened up in 1993.
We never really closed.

        
Edward Earl Young opened Clean Cut Barber Shop and Shoe Shine in 1993, in the then-controversial 5th Avenue neighborhood. That neighborhood became his home, and Clean Cut became part of its history.

        
He started it as a family business — it supported his family and put four children through to the other side. Three decades on, Clean Cut is still one of the largest barber shops in Gainesville, and still offers every one of the original services, including an old-fashioned shoe shine.
